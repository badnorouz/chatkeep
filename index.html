Is Chatkeep really free?

Yes. Completely — no trial, no subscription, no ads and no account. You can use it as much as you like.

Is my data private?

Your chats stay on your computer. Chatkeep has no servers, no analytics and no cloud. The only network access is downloading the files from your own claude.ai export e-mail, when you ask it to.

What does it work with?

Claude Code, Cowork and the Claude desktop app are read straight from your computer. claude.ai chats come in through Settings → Privacy → Export data, which Chatkeep imports for you.
